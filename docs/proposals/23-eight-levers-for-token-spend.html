23 · accepted · Eight levers for token spend

Proposal 23 · accepted 2026-09-15. The sponsor asked why building simple features costs so many tokens. A transcript measurement found where the spend goes, eight levers were offered on a decision page, and he picked one option per lever. This proposal records those picks and turns them into ledger items.

The ask

I would like you to analyze the Photo Vault app and Photo Vault engine and also the common rules chat v1. analyze where the tokens are getting spent and why it's getting so expensive to build simple features. I would like to optimize things. The sponsor, 14 September 2026, in the Common Rules rework session (its transcript).
Can you make a visual proposal out of that? What does it mean and what are the other options there? I don't want to read too much text and then I can decide if the strategy needs to be different. The sponsor, 14 September 2026, same session, after the Where the Tokens Go charts.

What was found

  • Leads carry about 500k tokens into every call: 66% (App), 67% (Engine) and 56% (Common Rules chat v1) of lead spend happened with more than 500k tokens in context. Measured from the three transcripts, each API response counted once.
  • Cache re-reads are 68–79% of lead spend; output is 10–15%.
  • Ledger, tracker and ruflo work is the lead's largest line: 33% (App), 23% (Engine), 42% (common-rules).
  • Nearly every item gets build plus review, and sometimes fix, agents. Items needing more than one agent carried 98% (App) and 92% (common-rules) of subagent spend.
  • Build, test and receipt runs are 62% of App subagent spend; Engine agents spend 53% reading and inspecting code.
  • Not verified: the per-step split divides a response's cost evenly across its tool calls; dollar figures are API list-price equivalents, not a bill.

Options

Each lever offered a recommended option, one to three alternatives, and "keep as is". The full set is on the Eight Levers page; the picked option is shown here.

L1 · Fresh leads

A · A fresh lead per milestone or day, under about 150k context, handed over by warmup and checkpoint.

L2 · Review for risk

A · A separate reviewer only for money, data safety, cross-cutting changes and releases.

L3 · Quiet builds

A · Output to a file; agents read only the summary line.

L4 · Bookkeeping out of the lead

A · One-line tracker commands instead of inline python on ledger JSON.

L5 · Bundle small issues

A · By surface: one agent, one commit per issue, one gate, one PR.

L6 · Context pack

B · A Haiku scout writes the pack before the builder starts.

L7 · Screenshots

Keep as is: the lead reads receipt images itself.

L8 · Models by role

Keep as is, and rethink sizing by value and story points first (proposal 25).

Recommended: A for L1 to L8, except where "keep" protects a rule the sponsor holds. He took the recommendation on L1 to L5, the alternative on L6, and kept L7 and L8.
