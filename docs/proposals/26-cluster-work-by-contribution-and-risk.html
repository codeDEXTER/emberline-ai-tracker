26 · proposed · Cluster work by contribution and risk

Proposal 26 · proposed 2026-09-15. Every queued change gets a contribution share and a risk score. The changes that together make up 90% of the value are built now; the last 10% is batched daily, weekly or when its surface is next touched, and stays tracked. Reviews and full test runs happen once per cluster, not once per item. Amends proposal 25, which is accepted but not built.

The ask

So if the reviews and all that can be also done in clusters so reviews and main testing, so can you think of some kind of a formula where we can analyse and see what is the percentage of contribution by these changes in the overall changes, what might be the impact what might be the risk? Is it a user visible risk, or is it a pack and risk? Does it completely stop working? Everything? Does it improve something and based on the different priorities and different percentage of contribution and percentage of risk it prioritise how to cluster these tasks. So lower ones can be done like once in a day or once in a week or when something else is done, can be deferred for later. At least I want to get the 90% of the work done rather than focusing on the last 10% and the majority is completed, but things are tracked. The sponsor, 15 September 2026, in the Common Rules rework session; proposal 26 ask A-01.

What was found

  • Not implemented anywhere. Proposal 25 (accepted 15 September, not built) adds value, points and a risk class to each item and routes review depth and bundling from them. It has no contribution share, no failure-impact scale, no 90% cut line and no deferral cadence.
  • Reviews and gates run per item today. Proposal 23's seven levers landed as one bundle but got one merge gate and one review, then ruflo-item done ran the full suite seven more times, about 45 minutes, in a checkout 14 commits behind main.
  • Change sizes are uneven. Proposal 23 by lines changed: tracker set/ask 551 (37%), bin/quiet 388 (26%), rules and templates 315 (21%), lead fixes 232 (16%). Two changes carried 63% of the work.
  • Not verified: that 90% of value sits in a minority of items for every project. Lines are a stand-in here; the formula uses value times points.

Options

A · Score, cut at 90%, batch the tail

Each change: share = value × points ÷ the queue's total; impact if it fails, 1–4; likelihood, 1–3; risk = impact × likelihood. Sort by share; the changes reaching 90% cumulative are built now, the rest go to daily, weekly or when-touched batches by risk. Costs: two fields and a scorer on top of proposal 25.

B · Risk-only lanes

No contribution share. High risk now, medium daily, low weekly. Costs: least. Rules out: finishing the valuable 90% first; a large valuable change with low risk waits a week.

C · Sponsor ranks, fixed cadence

You order the queue; the top ten each day are built, everything else goes weekly. Costs: your time daily. Rules out: risk shaping when things get reviewed.

Recommended: A. It is the only option that uses both numbers you asked for, share of the work and risk, and it keeps every deferred change on the board with a date.

Decisions

Each numbered line is one thing the sponsor answers. Keep the numbers stable once shown: answers and ledger items cite them.

  1. D1. Scheme: A (share, impact × likelihood, 90% cut, batched tail), B (risk-only lanes) or C (you rank, fixed cadence)?
  2. D2. Share of the work: value × points (from proposal 25), lines changed, or your own ranking?
  3. D3. Cut line for "now": 90%, 80% or 95% of the queue's share?
  4. D4. Tail cadence: risk 6+ daily, 3–5 weekly, 1–2 when touched; or the whole tail weekly; or the whole tail when touched?
  5. D5. Reviews and the full test suite once per bundle or batch, including one ruflo-item done for several items, with risk-9+ changes still alone; or keep per item?
  6. D6. Deferred changes re-scored after 30 days, 14 days, or never automatically?
