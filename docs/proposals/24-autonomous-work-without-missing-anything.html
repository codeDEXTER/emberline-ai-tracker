The ask

how we will ensure nothing important is missed. make a separate proposal. also the work needs to continue autonomsly. The sponsor, 14 September 2026 at 23:53 CEST, as his note on lever L1 of the Eight Levers page (database collection picks, document L1); recorded as proposal 23, ask A-10.

What was found

  • What a lead holds that a fresh lead must not lose: sponsor messages not yet recorded as asks, agents still running and their worktrees, findings in agent reports, notes left on published pages, and the next action.
  • The standard already carries most of it on disk: HANDOFF.md, the ledger, tracker checkpoint, and /warmup, which reads them in a fixed order. The gap is the moment of ending: nothing checks those files are complete before a lead stops.
  • Compaction summaries are paraphrase. On 4 September a summary's wording was quoted as the sponsor's words ten times in photo-vault. A handover must be the files, not the summary.
  • Agents report back to the session that started them. If a lead ends while an agent runs, the report arrives nowhere unless the handover names the agent and its worktree and the next lead reads the branch.
  • Not verified: whether a lead can start the next lead session without a click from the sponsor. The desktop app offers messages to existing sessions and scheduled tasks; neither has been tried for this.

Options

A · Closing check, then a new lead

A lead may end only after bin/handover --check passes: every sponsor message since it started has an ask row, every running agent is in the ledger with its worktree, the checkpoint matches the ledger, and warmup --check reads ready. The next lead starts from /warmup.

Costs: one new tool with tests. Rules out: ending a lead mid-item without a record.

B · Thin dispatcher, short item leads

One long-lived dispatcher holds only the queue: ledger, asks, and which bundle runs where. It never reads diffs, logs or images, so its context stays small. Each bundle gets its own short lead that merges and ends. The closing check from A runs when each item lead ends.

Costs: a new role and brief. Rules out: the dispatcher doing review or checks itself.

C · Same session, clear and warm up

No new session. At about 150k context the lead writes the checkpoint, clears its context and runs /warmup. Autonomy is untouched because the session never ends.

Costs: little. Rules out nothing, but depends on the lead being able to clear itself, which is not verified.

Recommended: B with A's closing check. The dispatcher is the one thing that never ends, so work keeps moving, and every short lead is checked before it stops, so nothing is dropped.
